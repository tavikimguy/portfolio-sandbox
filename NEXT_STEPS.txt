================================================================================
❓ COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: Do I need Cloudflare for local development?
A: No! Everything works locally. D1 is only needed for production persistence.

Q: Can I test drawing without Cloudflare?
A: Yes. Drawings render locally on the canvas. Press Ctrl+Shift+I to see
   "POST /api/annotations" requests (will 404 locally without backend).

Q: How do I change canvas size?
A: Edit CANVAS_WIDTH & CANVAS_HEIGHT in src/lib/portfolio-cards.ts

Q: Can multiple people use it at the same time?
A: Yes, but they won't see each other's cursor (that requires WebSocket/Phase 2).
   They'll see the same annotations after the 5s poll.

Q: What about user authentication?
A: Not implemented yet. Everyone sees the same canvas.
   Could add IP-based or name-based tagging if needed.
